Built by a geologist, for independents

Since finishing my PhD at Tulane, I've wanted to prospect independently, but traditional subsurface mapping software costs thousands of dollars a year in licensing. So I built GeoPicks for geologists, independents, and consultants who map from well control. It reads data from SONRIS, the Texas RRC, IHS/Enverus, Petra and Kingdom exports. Seismic integration is on the roadmap.
